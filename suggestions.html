Web Presence Suggestions

This page outlines specific suggestions to make your portfolio more effective for recruiters. Recruiters often spend less than 10 seconds scanning a profile. The goal is to make your skills and impact immediately visible.

1. Visualize Your Skills

Instead of hiding skills in paragraphs, use a "Skills Matrix" or tag cloud. This allows recruiters to verify your tech stack at a glance.

2. Use the STAR Method for Projects

Your current project links are minimal. Expand them into "Case Studies" using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Quantify your impact whenever possible.

Example Project: Deep Q-Network Cart-Pole

Situation: Needed to understand reinforcement learning concepts applied to control systems.

Task: Implement an agent capable of balancing a pole on a cart for 500+ frames.

Action: Built a Deep Q-Network (DQN) using PyTorch, implementing experience replay and target networks to stabilize training.

Result: Achieved optimal performance 20% faster than standard benchmarks; code used as a reference for peer learning.

3. Add Social Proof

If you have recommendations on LinkedIn or from professors, feature 1-2 short quotes. It builds trust immediately.

"Jack brings a unique perspective by combining psychological insights with technical rigor. He was instrumental in our data analysis project..."

4. Optimize Call-to-Actions (CTAs)

Ensure your Resume/CV is downloadable from the navigation bar or a sticky button, not just buried in the "About" text.
